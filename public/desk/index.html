How it works
1. We rank every company by how likely it is to need an M&A advisor right now. Hot ones are at the top.
2. Each one is scored against every buyer mandate (who they buy, where, and at what size). Charts show who fits and whether one buyer stands out.
3. The Today list tells you the one next thing to do for each owner. Click the button.
4. Log the owner's reply and we qualify it. Interested owners get a first call with an advisor and, after a few meetings, sign an engagement letter: the mandate.
